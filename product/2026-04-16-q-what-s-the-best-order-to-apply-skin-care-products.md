Great question. It’s one we get often. There are a lot of new products out there and figuring out which ones to use and in what order isn’t easy.

The simplest answer to your question is that you should always apply products to your face in order of most liquid to least. If you’re using a, serum, moisturizer and eye cream daily, you’d use them in that order. The goal is to trap moisture beneath the surface, which will help keep your skin hydrated (i.e. younger-looking). Applying the more liquid products first will allow them to move deeper into the skin and better accomplish their goal. Thicker moisturizers, gels and eye creams provide a protective outer layer and lock in the active ingredients.

[Care Products](https://memorycircuits.hbsnzj.com/)

A lot of readers ask: “Why use a serum?” The answer is that most contain more concentrated doses of vitamins and antioxidants, which help nourish the skin and improve collagen production—which is what keeps your skin plump and wrinkle-free. Think of your skin like a rubber band. If you keep it sealed in a bag, hidden in a drawer, it’ll remain bouncy and elastic for years. But leave a rubber band in the sun for a few days and it gets dry and brittle. Serums and moisturizers are what keep your skin bouncy and elastic. (Drinking plenty of water daily doesn’t hurt either.)

Suggested Routine

We recommend washing your face daily with a cleanser. Soap will clean your skin, but it’ll also dry it out. Remember: dry skin equals old skin. Cleansers cleanse, but don’t dry. We’re using Gillette Fusion Sensitive Face Wash right now.

A few times a week, use a face scrub to slough away the dead skin cells that build up daily. You can’t see them, but that’s what clogs your pores and leaves your skin looking dull. Try Bulldog Original Face Scrub.

After that, apply a serum, moisturizer (with SPF) and eye cream daily. Here’s what we’re using now:

At night, you have options. If nothing else, wash your face before you go to bed. We’re not big fans of slathering on tons of products at night. This is a good time for your skin to breathe. But applying either a moisturizer or the same serum you use in the morning isn’t a bad idea.

Extra Credit

If you are prone to oily skin or large pores, we’d suggest using an at-home peel from time to time. Don’t be scared by the word “peel.” Think of it as a more intense exfoliation than you’d get from using a scrub. Most are simply pads infused with natural fruit acids, which help keep your skin smooth, and wrinkles at bay. Our go-to peel is Dr. Dennis Gross Skincare Alpha Beta Peel. It’s a quick, two-step process of that involves swiping a pad across your face a few times. Let it sit for a few minutes then neutralize it with a second pad.
