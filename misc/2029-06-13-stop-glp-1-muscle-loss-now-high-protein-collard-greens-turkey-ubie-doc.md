1. Understanding GLP-1 and Its Role

What is GLP-1?Glucagon-like peptide-1 is a hormone your gut releases in response to food. Medications that mimic GLP-1:• Slow gastric emptying (you feel full longer)• Enhance insulin release when blood sugar is high• Suppress excess glucagon (a hormone that raises blood sugar)

Why pair GLP-1 therapy with the right foods?While GLP-1 meds help curb appetite, choosing nutrient-dense, high-protein meals:• Preserves lean muscle mass• Supports metabolic health• Helps you meet protein goals without excess calories

7. Tips to Maximize Success

Track Protein Intake: Aim for 20–30 g lean protein per meal.

Plan Ahead: Batch-cook turkey and pre-chop greens for quick reheating.

Spice It Up: Use garlic, ginger, smoked paprika, cumin, or cayenne to keep flavors vibrant without extra salt.

Balance Your Plate: Pair your turkey and greens with a small portion of whole grains or starchy veggies if you need additional carbs.

8. Monitoring, Safety, and When to Seek Help

Even with the best recipes, everyone's response to GLP-1 therapy varies. Keep an eye on:

Blood sugar readings (if diabetic) to catch any lows or highs early

Gastrointestinal symptoms—occasional mild nausea is common, but persistent vomiting or severe pain needs prompt attention
