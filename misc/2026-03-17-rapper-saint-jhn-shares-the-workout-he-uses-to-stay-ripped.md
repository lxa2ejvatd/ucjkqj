Just take a look at the artist's Instagram page, or watch any of his videos or performances that have dropped ahead of his album While the World Was Burning, which just dropped today. Sure, sometimes he'll rock a hoodie, or throw on a jacket—but you'll almost always see him showing off his torso with hard-earned pecs, abs, and arm muscles.

He'll be the first to admit that those muscles are largely for show. "My fitness goals are simple: I wanna be sexy," he says to kick off a group workout he put on for the MH crew to show off his training regimen. Surprisingly enough, he's wearing a shirt when he says this. But that doesn't last long. By the time he's done warming up, the cutoff is nowhere to be seen.

From there, the rapper walks through a hybrid boxing/strength training session with his friends that requires him to go from throwing punches to performing ab techniques on the ground. But SAINt JHN admits that there is more to his training than pure aesthetics.

"Being a performing artist, rhythm is key," he says. [Boxing] helps your breath control. It helps your stability. Imagine you're on tour and you're jumping up and down for 45 minutes or 72 minutes a night. You need the clarity, the breath control, and the stamina to perform it."
