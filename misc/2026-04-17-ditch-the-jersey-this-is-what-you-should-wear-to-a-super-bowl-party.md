# Ditch The Jersey. This Is What You Should Wear to a Super Bowl Party

[Wear to Super Bowl](https://repair-cascade.jcesqc8.com/blog/6734206135.html)

[Super Bowl Party](https://appetitecode.jcesqc8.com/blog/6900658751.html)

[Ditch The Jersey This](https://repair-cascade.jcesqc8.com/)

We know, we know. You love your favorite football player. But can’t you respect and support him without slapping his name across your back? We can let that look slide—medium-wash jeans and an oversized polyester jersey—for a young fan. But once you’ve hit adulthood, it’s time to put those away for safekeeping.

Instead, show your team some love by adding just a touch of spirit to an otherwise socially acceptable outfit. We’re partial to a team beanie; it let’s you show off your fandom without disrupting the rest of your outfit. This knit one from '47 will also keep you warm on your commute to the party.

If you’re headed to a Super Bowl a house or even a low-key bar, you can still get away with a casual outfit. Just because you’re not wearing a jersey doesn’t mean you need to wear a suit. Layering a hoodie under an overcoat is actually one of the more polished ways to pull off loungewear. Just make sure the hoodie isn’t the one you also wear to warm up before a workout. A fresh gray one looks laidback but stylish under a camel topcoat.

Pair this with equally modern pants and sneakers. A lightly distressed gray pair, like these from All Saints, look cool paired with the hoodie and coat. We mean lightly, too—an overly ripped or frayed pair of jeans just looks sloppy. Complete the look with a clean white sneaker. These high-top leather ones from Koio pop against the gray outfit.
