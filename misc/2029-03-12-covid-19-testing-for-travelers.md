COVID-19 Testing For Travelers

What can you expect from COVID-19 testing for travelers with Drip Hydration?

Our tests determine whether you are currently infected with the COVID-19 virus. Our certified nurses come to your location to administer a Rapid Antigen or Rapid PCR test and issue you the necessary certificate for traveling (pending a negative test result).

About COVID-19 Testing For Traveling:

Method: Nasal swab

Recommended Type: Rapid PCR or Rapid Antigen

Collected by certified nurses

Collected at your location (home, office, hotel)

Accurate & fast results

Travel Certificate is included

When To Get A COVID-19 Test For Traveling

You should get a COVID-19 test for traveling if you:

Need the certificate for traveling from or to a country that requires a COVID-19 Certificate.

Are not vaccinated and have arrived in the US in the past 72 hours.

Are vaccinated but start feeling symptoms (fever, dry cough, tiredness, loss of taste or smell, shortness of breath) within a week of your trip.

Are vaccinated, but your destination requires a negative PCR test COVID-19 Certificate.

Need results the same day for travel.

Need a rapid PCR test to avoid self-isolation at your destination.
