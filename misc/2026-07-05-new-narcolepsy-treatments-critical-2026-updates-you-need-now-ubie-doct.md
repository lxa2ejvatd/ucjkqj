Recent advancements in narcolepsy treatment include a new lower-sodium oxybate formulation, which reduces cardiovascular risk, and improved medications like pitolisant and solriamfetol that target sleep-wake regulation more precisely. Emerging orexin receptor agonists also show promise for addressing the underlying cause of narcolepsy type 1.

If you're experiencing excessive daytime sleepiness, sudden muscle weakness, or disrupted nighttime sleep, understanding your symptoms is the critical first step. Narcolepsy is a condition that causes people to suddenly fall asleep. If you're experiencing unusual sleep patterns or sudden episodes of falling asleep during the day, try Ubie's free AI-powered symptom checker to better understand your symptoms. Recent advances in treatment focus on making medications safer and more effective.

Lower-Sodium Oxybate: A new formulation of oxybate has been developed with less sodium. This is important because too much sodium can be bad for the heart and blood pressure. This new version helps people with narcolepsy without adding extra sodium to their diet.

Medications for Sleep Regulation: New medicines are being studied that help control sleep patterns better. These medications aim to help people stay awake during the day and sleep better at night.
