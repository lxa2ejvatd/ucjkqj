About this lesson

Master midface and tear trough enhancement with Dr. Juan Sopena’s expert approach to restoring volume and creating natural facial contours.

This intermediate lesson focuses on facial assessment, zygomatic mapping, and safe deep filler placement using Teoxane RHA® 2 and RHA® 4 to improve cheek projection and under-eye hollowing.You’ll learn structured injection protocols for the malar and tear trough areas, with guidance on fat pad restoration, precise product selection, and depth control.

Combining needle and cannula techniques, this training emphasizes anatomical safety, precision, and naturally balanced outcomes-ideal for practitioners ready to refine their midface rejuvenation skills and master the indirect approach to tear trough correction.

How to combine lateral and medial mid-face correction for natural lift and contour.
