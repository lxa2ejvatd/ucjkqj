# The Brilliant Single-Leg Deadlift Hack We Wish We Had Known About Sooner

The single-leg deadlift is a great exercise for improving your balance and stability.

[About Sooner](https://collagen-atelier.bbsxyxy.com/)

[Deadlift Hack We](https://allpepay.com/)

In fact, it’s so challenging for your balance that it’s nearly impossible to do the exercise with added weight in order to build strength, says Men’s Health Fitness Director B.J. Gaddour.

But Gaddour has a hack for that: By anchoring one foot on a bench, you’ll get just enough help balancing to allow you to load up the exercise.
