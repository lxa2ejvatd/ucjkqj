One of our nurses are available during the detox process. During this time, they will:

Monitor the patient’s progress

Adjust the detox plan as needed to match the patient’s needs

Administer medications to help minimize discomfort from withdrawal symptoms

Perform lab tests

Administer IVs as needed

Provide coaching through withdrawal symptoms to help patients successfully complete detox

We are dedicated to helping our patients successfully and safely complete this essential step of addiction recovery.
