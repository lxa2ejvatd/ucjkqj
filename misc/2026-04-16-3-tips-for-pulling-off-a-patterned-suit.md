HOW TO PICK THE RIGHT PATTERN FOR YOUR SUIT

If this is your first foray into wearing a patterned suit, opt for small prints, like a simple plaid or check. We like this one from Suit Supply ($499, suitsupply.com).

Go with tonal patterns—i.e. charcoal on light gray, medium blue on navy—which are naturally more understated, like this Ted Baker trim fit ($695, nordstrom.com).

Rule of thumb: Small, understated patterns look sophisticated and dressy. Larger prints are more casual, and not as accommodating to pattern newbies.

STYLE YOUR PATTERNED SUIT THE RIGHT WAY

A patterned suit will make you stand out, but make sure it’s for the right reasons.

The suit should be your focal point; if you have too many eye-catching parts to your outfit, you’ll just look sloppy.

When it comes to picking a shirt to pair with your suit, keep it simple: You can never go wrong with a clean, white dress shirt.

Patterned suits also work well with patterned shirts—but there’s a catch. If your suit uses a small pattern (like thin stripes), you’ll need to wear a shirt with a large one (like an oversized floral).

WEAR YOUR PATTERNED SUIT WITH CONFIDENCE

Once you’ve mastered the subtle prints, take it up a notch. Try something exciting like this palm print suit from ASOS ($138 for the jacket asos.com).

Or snag a patterned jacket, but pair it with a solid-color shirt and bottoms.
