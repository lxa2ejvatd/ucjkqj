Explanation

When you're looking at food labels to find out if erythritol is included, you should look at the ingredients list. Erythritol will be listed by its name just like other ingredients. It is often used in products like ice cream or frozen desserts.

Tips for Checking Labels

Read the Ingredients List: This is where erythritol will be listed if it is used in the product.

Check for Other Sweeteners: Sometimes, food products use a combination of sweeteners, so look for erythritol among others.
