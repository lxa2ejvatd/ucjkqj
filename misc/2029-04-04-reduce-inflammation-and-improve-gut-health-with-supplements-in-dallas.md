Recognizing Gut Inflammation

Gut inflammation, the immune response of the gastrointestinal tract to harmful stimuli, can be triggered by genetics, inadequate diet, unmanaged stress, lack of sleep, excessive alcohol consumption, and specific medications. Diets rich in processed foods, sugar, and unhealthy fats exacerbate this condition.

Diarrhea, bloating, weight loss, stomach pain, and fatigue are some of the primary symptoms of gut inflammation. Chronic inflammation heightens the risk of severe gastrointestinal problems and can have a negative impact on mental health, emphasizing the importance of appropriate gut health supplements and lifestyle therapies.

How Diet Impacts Gut Health

Optimal gut health depends on nutrition, with certain foods causing inflammation and others reducing it.
