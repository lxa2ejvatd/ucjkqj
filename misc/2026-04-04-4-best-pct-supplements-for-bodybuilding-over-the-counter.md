As you cycle with exogenous testosterone, both your Luteinizing Hormone (LH) and Follicle-Stimulating Hormone (FSH) will begin to lower. Ashwagandha root has been shown to not only increase LH, but also free testosterone as well. Some studies even show it having positive effects on FSH, prolactin, and semen profiles in the male body.
