What can I expect during a NAD IV treatment?

Our nurse will arrive at your location of choice with all the equipment necessary for the appointment. They will then administer the IV into your arm and stay with you to monitor the treatment and answer any questions you may have. We recommend choosing a comfortable place for NAD treatments for your infusion. You can sit back, watch a movie or a favorite show, browse social media, or even work while the treatment is in progress.

Which type of NAD treatment is right for me?

It depends on your health goals and lifestyle: IV NAD Therapy is best for rapid, high-dose cellular support and deep detox. NAD Injections are great for ongoing maintenance and easy self-administration at home. NAD Nasal Spray is ideal for convenience and daily mental clarity support. A medical team member can help guide you to the right choice during your initial consultation.

Do I need a prescription or consultation?

Yes. All NAD treatments require a medical intake or virtual consultation to determine eligibility and ensure your safety. Once approved, you can begin your treatment plan.

How quickly will I feel results?

Some patients feel energized and mentally sharper within hours. Others may need a few sessions before noticing full effects, especially when addressing long-term fatigue, brain fog, or burnout.

Can I combine NAD with other treatments or vitamins?

Yes. NAD can be safely combined with vitamins like B-complex, Vitamin C, Glutathione, and more to enhance results. Many NAD plans include these as part of their formulation.
