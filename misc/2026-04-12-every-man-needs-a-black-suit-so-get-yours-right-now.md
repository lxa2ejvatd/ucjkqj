What to Look for in Black Suits for Men

Fit and fabric are the top priorities for any color suit, but none more so than black. It doesn’t matter if you go with a soft shoulder or fully padded, a slim fit or boxy fit, tailored pants or wide leg, as long as fit and proportions complement your body then you’re good to go. If you are unsure of your side, most brand websites have a size chart. Which is always good to reference anyway, since sizing can fluctuate from brand to brand.

For maximum versatility, stick to a black suit that checks all the fundamental boxes: two button front, notch lapel, double vent, and flat front trousers. Smaller details can vary, but as long as you have those classic suit elements included, you can do anything you want with your black suit.

What to Wear With a Black Suit for Men?

Full black and white is definitely a high fashion look, but off the runway, you run the risk of looking like you’re in Men in Black. Think outside the “professional uniform” box by mixing up your shirt, shoes, and accessories. Rock an oxford shirt: top button unbuttoned, a bold tie, and a flashy gold watch for the office. Go high-low with a hoodie and your favorite running shoes for a night out. Despite what you may have heard, you absolutely can wear brown shoes with a black suit, but it is a delicate balancing act so proceed with caution. When in doubt, put on a chambray or denim shirt.

Are Black Suits Too Formal?

Black suits are absolutely not too formal. They have a reputation for formality because black is just the easiest color to use for work uniforms and the best color for blending in. Black is also the color of choice for pretty much every high fashion brand ever. However, that doesn't mean you can’t make black suits light hearted and fun. Anything you can do with a navy suit, you can do with a black suit. Ironically, you will stand out by going semi-casual in a black suit because most men haven’t tried it yet. Once you do though, you'll have it in your repertoire for life.
