What to Consider

Fabric

Just as with t-shirts, high quality cotton is the best overall fabric because it can handle all the things you want a henley to do. The natural fiber is breathable and helps regulate perspiration and body heat. It starts out soft and gets better with age. Best of all, it is easy to wash since it is machine-and dryer-friendly.

For added flexibility and athletic performance, look for a cotton blended with synthetic fibers like polyester or spandex. These offer a snugger fit that will move with your body, as well as alleviating some ripping and tearing risk.

Fit

A good way to decide how your henley should fit is to match whatever fit you prefer in a t-shirt. If you have broader shoulders and like a boxy fitting tee, go with a classic or relaxed fit henley. On the other hand, if you are lean and prefer a form fitting look, go with a slim fit.

One thing to keep in mind is that a looser fitting henley is preferable for warmer weather. When the fabric drapes loosely, it allows for more air flow. All the buttons can come undone on hot days to really cool off.

Buttons

Two or three buttons are the standard for henleys. There are single button designs out there, but that kind of defeats the purpose. Shirts that come with four or five buttons have a deeper cut opening, which you probably won’t utilize often. For most outfits, a three button henley with the top one or two buttons left open provides the best look.

How do you style a henley shirt?

The answer depends on the particular henley. All henleys can be undershirts, or long underwear if they are thermal, but many are not refined enough to stand alone as a shirt. Fit and fabric are the two main factors that decide if it should be worn solo. For fit, you don’t want the shirt to be too tight or too loose. If it is form fitting, like a compression shirt, that is not a good look for a shirt. On the other hand, henleys are not the type of shirt that can look good with a baggy fit - like a hoodie or jacket.

Henleys are an acceptable stand in for a collared shirt, as long as the fit is right and the fabric is crisp. For example, you can wear a white henley under a blazer with jeans and loafers. This is a comfortable elevated look for social occasions and most offices. Just make sure that the henley is a nice crisp white and, if it comes out of the wash wrinkled, give it a once over with an iron.

As for the fabric, you want a solid color that is either smooth or has a very fine waffle texture. Most cotton options work but you should also check to make sure the fabric isn’t fine enough to be transparent, especially with white.
