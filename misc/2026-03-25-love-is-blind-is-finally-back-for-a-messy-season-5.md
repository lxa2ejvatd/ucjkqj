# ‘Love Is Blind’ Is Finally Back for a Messy Season 5

[Finally Back for Messy](https://thymosin-report.hbsnzj.com/blog/1293009006.html)

Love Is Blind Season 5 is finally here. Starting September 22, a whole new cast of singles will be placed in pods and try to find a partner, sight unseen. It's one of Netflix's biggest shows, and potentially one of the easiest to shoot, as all it takes is a group of regular people, one location for the majority of shooting, and a few appearances from the hosts. As long as Netflix continues renewing the series, Love Is Blind can film seasons back-to-back in various cities until they match up (or cause drama) between every eligible bachelor and bachelorette in the United States.

If previous seasons are any indication, there's bound to be tears of both joy and pain, yelling, and, hopefully, a few happy marriages at the end of the season. Despite only seeing each other after they get engaged in the pods, contestants still manage to get involved in love triangles, get engaged and then break up, or have issues with other members of the cast.

Season 4 gave us all of that and more, with two contestants deemed "mean girls" and complicated relationships only getting more complicated as people exited the pods. There's no telling what Season 5 will bring, but Netflix will undoubtedly do what it can to cast a mix of heartwarming singles we can root for and cast members who bring more chaos than romance.
