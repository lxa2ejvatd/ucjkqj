Full Bio

Dr. Resham Uttamchandani is a double board-certified physician in Family Medicine and Obesity Medicine with a focused practice in longevity, metabolic health, and personalized preventive care. She specializes in optimizing the core physiologic drivers of healthspan, including metabolic flexibility, hormonal balance, mitochondrial function, and nervous system regulation. Her goal is to help patients achieve sustained vitality through individualized, proactive care tailored to their unique needs. Dr. Uttamchandani earned her Bachelor of Science in Public Health from George Washington University before receiving her Doctor of Medicine from the American University of the Caribbean. She completed her Family Medicine residency at Eisenhower Medical Center, where she developed a strong foundation in comprehensive primary care and preventive medicine. She is also a recognized voice in longevity and preventive health, contributing to publications including Forbes Health, Newsweek, BestLife, Parsley Health, Mission Matters, Delish, and NewsBreak. Her approach combines evidence-based medicine, lifestyle interventions, and personalized treatment strategies designed to improve both lifespan and healthspan.
