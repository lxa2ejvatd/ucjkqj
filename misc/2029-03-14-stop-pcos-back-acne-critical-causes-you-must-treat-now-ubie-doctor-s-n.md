Cystic acne on the back, especially in individuals with Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS), can be caused by a combination of factors. One important factor is hormonal imbalance, particularly increased levels of androgens, which are hormones that can make the skin produce more oil. This extra oil can clog pores, leading to acne.

Another factor is inflammation. Acne can be seen as an immune response, where the body reacts to clogged pores and bacteria by causing inflammation, leading to cystic acne. This inflammation can make acne more severe and harder to treat.

Here are some key points:

Hormones: High levels of androgens can increase oil production in the skin, causing clogged pores.

Inflammation: The body's immune response can cause inflammation, making acne worse.
