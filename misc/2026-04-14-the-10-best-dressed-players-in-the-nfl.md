# The 10 Best Dressed Players In the NFL

Many NFL players are as stylish off the field as they are electric on the gridiron. From impeccably tailored suits to professionally curated weekend wardrobes, these well-dressed ballers score huge style touchdowns.

Larry Fitzgerald

Cardinals receiver Larry Fitzgerald looks sharp in a slate suit and double monk dress shoes. We’d rock this look around the office, but maybe swap out the baby blue bags for a cool leather briefcase instead.

DeAndre Hopkins

This Texans wideout has an undone style that radiates coolness. Here, he’s wearing a sharp suit, but he dresses it down with a simple gray T-shirt.

Russell Wilson

The Seahawks signal caller knows how to make his bold suit stand out: He keeps the rest of his outfit simple with a black dress shirt and black patterned tie.

Victor Cruz

Victor Cruz is the master of suit-and-sneaker style.
