What is Gynecomastia?

A condition in which abnormal hormone levels cause males to develop female characteristics, such as breast enlargement. Common causes include medications and brain tumors.

Typical Symptoms of Gynecomastia

Enlarged male breast tissue

Breasts feel stretched, tight or full

Breast lumps

Nipples are very sensitive

Taking medication to increase urination

Nipple rash, redness, discharge, pain or itch

Pain on pressing the chest wall

Decreased sex drive

Diagnostic Questions for Gynecomastia

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Have you noticed an unusual increase in your breast size?

Do your breasts feel stretched or tight?

Have you noticed any lumps in your breasts?

Do your nipples feel uncomfortable, painful, or itchy when your clothes touch them?

Are you taking any diuretic medication?

Treatment of Gynecomastia

Treatment for this condition depends on the cause. Simple options include dressing less noticeably and losing weight. The doctor may prescribe hormone therapy to correct imbalances or surgery if tumors are the cause.
