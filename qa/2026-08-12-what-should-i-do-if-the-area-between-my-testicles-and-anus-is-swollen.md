Explanation

Swelling in the area between the testicles and anus may indicate an infection, such as streptococcal cellulitis. This type of infection can occur when bacteria enter through a break in the skin, leading to redness, warmth, and pain in the affected area. It is important to see a healthcare provider quickly to get the right treatment, which may include antibiotics.

Prompt medical care is essential to prevent complications and to ensure effective treatment.
