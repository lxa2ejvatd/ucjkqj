Explanation

Wegovy (semaglutide) is a once-weekly injectable medication approved for weight management in adults with obesity or who are overweight with at least one weight-related condition. For Black women over 50, the benefits of Wegovy—such as improved blood sugar control and reduced cardiovascular risk—must be balanced against specific health considerations. This guide covers what to watch, why it matters, and practical steps for safe, effective use of Wegovy for Black women over 50.

Why Wegovy?

Slows stomach emptying (increases fullness)

Reduces appetite signals in the brain

Helps regulate blood sugar levels

In clinical trials, people on Wegovy lost an average of 15% of their body weight over 68 weeks.

For Black women over 50—who face higher rates of hypertension, type 2 diabetes, and cardiovascular disease—weight loss can lower health risks.

Unique Considerations for Black Women Over 50

Higher Rates of Hypertension and Heart Disease– Monitor blood pressure closely; obesity and age increase cardiovascular risk.

Elevated Type 2 Diabetes Risk– Black women are diagnosed with diabetes at younger ages and have higher complication rates.

Bone Health & Menopause– After menopause, bone density can decline. Rapid weight loss may exacerbate this.

Kidney Function– Diabetes and high blood pressure can impair kidney function over time.

Cultural & Socioeconomic Factors– Access to fresh foods, care providers, and supportive communities can affect adherence.

What to Monitor Before Starting Wegovy

Prior to your first injection, get baseline measurements and labs. Share results with your provider:

Weight, Height & Body Mass Index (BMI)

Blood Pressure & Heart Rate

Fasting Blood Glucose & HbA1c– Assesses baseline diabetes risk or control.

Lipid Panel– Total cholesterol, LDL, HDL, triglycerides.

Kidney Function– Serum creatinine, estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR).

Liver Enzymes– ALT, AST to rule out liver disease.

Thyroid Function (TSH)– Rarely, GLP-1 agonists can unmask thyroid issues.

Vitamin D & Calcium– Key for bone health, especially post-menopause.

Baseline Mental Health Screening– Because of potential mood changes or appetite shifts.

Contact your healthcare provider or seek urgent care if you experience:
