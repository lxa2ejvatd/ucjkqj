Sharp pain in the thigh can be concerning. Two possible causes, based on the references, involve nerve-related issues.

Saphenous Neuritis: This involves irritation or damage to the saphenous nerve, which can cause pain along the inner part of the thigh. This condition is not well understood but is recognized as a cause of medial thigh pain.
