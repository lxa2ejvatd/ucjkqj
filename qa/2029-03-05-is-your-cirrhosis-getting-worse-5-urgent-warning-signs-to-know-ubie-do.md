How can I tell if my cirrhosis is getting worse?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Cirrhosis is worsening when new or intensifying symptoms appear, including:

Increased swelling in the abdomen (ascites) or legs (edema)

Explanation

Cirrhosis is a condition where the liver is severely scarred, and it can get worse over time. Some signs that cirrhosis might be worsening include more swelling in the belly or legs, feeling confused or sleepy, and new or more frequent bleeding from the gums or nose.

Key Signs to Watch For:

Swelling: Look for increased swelling in your abdomen or legs.

Confusion: If you start feeling confused or very sleepy, this could be a sign.

Bleeding: New or more frequent bleeding from your gums or nose can be a warning sign.
