Which antibiotics affect birth control pills?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Do antibiotics affect birth control? In most cases, no. The majority of antibiotics do not reduce the effectiveness of birth control pills. The main exception is rifampin (and related drugs like rifabutin), used to treat tuberculosis, which can lower hormone levels and make oral contraceptives less reliable. Common antibiotics like amoxicillin, penicillin, and doxycycline are considered safe to take with birth control.

Explanation

When it comes to birth control pills and antibiotics, most antibiotics do not interfere with the effectiveness of birth control pills. However, there is one exception: rifampin. Rifampin is an antibiotic used to treat certain bacterial infections, and it can make birth control pills less effective.

Key Points:

Rifampin: This antibiotic can lower the effectiveness of birth control pills, which means there is a higher chance of pregnancy if you are using birth control pills while taking rifampin.

Other Antibiotics: According to the studies, other common antibiotics do not have the same effect as rifampin. Most antibiotics will not change how well birth control pills work.
