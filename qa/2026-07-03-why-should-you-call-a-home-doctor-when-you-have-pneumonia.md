# Why Should You Call A Home Doctor When You Have Pneumonia?

25 Nov Why Should You Call A Home Doctor When You Have Pneumonia?

Pneumonia is an infection of your lungs that can be mild in some people and fatal in others. Since symptoms range so widely, it’s important to seek treatment to help prevent complications. But there are several other reasons you should consider a house call doctor for pneumonia. Let’s take a look.

Convenience

The last thing you want to do when you are sick is to make all the effort into going to a doctor’s office, including sitting in traffic and then sitting in a crowded waiting room. Booking an appointment with a house call doctor for pneumonia allows you to have a doctor’s visit in the comfort of your own home, allowing you more time to get the rest you need to recover.

Keeping others safe

Having a house call appointment also keeps you from spreading your pneumonia to others you would come into contact with at a doctor’s office. It also keeps you safe from contracting another illness, such as Covid-19, from another patient in the waiting room.

Telemedicine

Another option many house call doctors offer is to have an appointment via a telemedicine call. Telemedicine is a doctor’s appointment conducted via a phone call rather than an in-person visit.

Prescription refills

An additional benefit of having an appointment with a house call doctor is that you can have prescriptions refilled without having to go to a clinic in person.
