White discharge from the urethra can indeed be a sign of pus, which often suggests an underlying infection. Urethritis, an inflammation of the urethra, is one condition where this symptom might appear. Urethritis can be caused by various infections, including sexually transmitted infections (STIs).

Reference 1 discusses urethral trichomoniasis, a type of STI that can cause urethritis in men, leading to symptoms like discharge.

[sign of pus Ubie](https://skinsequence.escortskhi.com/blog/0949986421.html)

Reference 2 provides a review of urethritis, highlighting that symptoms can include discharge, burning, or itching.

Reference 3 suggests that clinical and microbiological investigations are important for identifying the specific cause of the symptoms.

If you or someone you know is experiencing these symptoms, it is important to seek medical advice for proper diagnosis and treatment. Early treatment can help prevent complications and spread to others.
