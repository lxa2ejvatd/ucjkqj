There are numerous health conditions that can cause a severe headache in the back of your head. Sometimes, this symptom can be a warning sign of a serious health condition.

Here are some steps you can take:

Seek Medical Attention: It is important to consult with a healthcare professional to determine the cause of your headache and receive appropriate treatment.

Monitor Symptoms: Keep track of any other symptoms you might be experiencing, such as dizziness or vision changes, and share these with your healthcare provider.
