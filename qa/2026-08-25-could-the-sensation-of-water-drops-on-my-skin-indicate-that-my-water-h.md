Explanation

When a pregnant person’s "water breaks," it means the bag of fluid around the baby has ruptured. Sometimes, this can make you feel dampness or water drops on your skin, especially around the lower belly or between your legs. However, not every wet sensation is due to your water breaking. It could also be normal vaginal discharge or even sweat. Typically, if your water has broken, you might notice a steady flow or a large gush of clear, watery fluid. The fluid is usually odorless and not stained. If you experience such signs or if you are unsure whether what you are feeling is amniotic fluid, it is very important to speak with your doctor. They can do tests to check if your water has broken and make sure that you and your baby are safe. Paying close attention to any changes and getting timely help is the best way to stay healthy during pregnancy.
