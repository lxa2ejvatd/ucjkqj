# Why Get A Rapid Covid-19 Test Before Traveling?

06 Nov Why Get A Rapid Covid-19 Test Before Traveling?

Travel during Covid-19 presents a complicated challenge for even the most seasoned traveler with the new regulations and requirements which surround the pandemic response. Covid-19 testing can bring peace of mind to your traveling experience. You will know that you aren’t sick and won’t spread the virus.

Technically, there are no rules indicating that you need to get tested before you board a plane or hop in a car for extended travel. However, testing before you travel can help ensure that you do not have Covid-19 and that you do not accidentally spread it to others over the course of your travels.

When should you get tested for Covid-19?

If you think you’ve been exposed to someone with Covid-19, or have noticed flu-like symptoms consistent with the symptoms of Covid-19, it’s recommended that you get tested.

If you have been in contact with someone with Covid-19 but do not have any symptoms, testing can help determine if you are an asymptomatic carrier (someone who has the virus but does not show symptoms) to help prevent others from getting sick.

How quickly will you get the results?

Rapid COVID antigen tests return results over the course of 15 minutes.
