Can I stop PPI suddenly?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Should you stop taking proton pump inhibitors (PPIs) suddenly? No. Abruptly stopping PPIs is not recommended because it can cause rebound acid hypersecretion, leading to a return or worsening of symptoms like heartburn and acid reflux. Instead, doctors typically recommend gradually tapering the dose over several weeks to allow your stomach's acid production to normalize safely.
