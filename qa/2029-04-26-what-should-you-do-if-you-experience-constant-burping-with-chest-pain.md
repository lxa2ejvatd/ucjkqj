Constant burping with chest pain can be a sign of different health issues. It might be related to heart problems, like angina, which is when your heart does not get enough blood. This can cause chest pain and sometimes burping, as shown in some studies.

Some people with heart problems experience burping as a symptom. It may be an unusual symptom of angina or other heart conditions.

[do if you](https://insulin-pathways.hbsnzj.com/blog/8454756451.html)

Burping can also occur if you have gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD). This is when stomach acid comes up into your throat, causing discomfort and burping.
