Explanation

Lower back pain that makes it hard to stand up straight can be due to several reasons, including changes in your spine after surgery or from standing too long.

Possible Causes:

Spinal Fusion: Sometimes, after a spinal fusion, the spine can become too flat, leading to pain and difficulty standing straight.

Prolonged Standing: Standing for long periods can also cause pain, possibly due to how your body reacts to standing.

Management Options:

Physical Therapy: This can help strengthen your back and improve your posture.

Tailored Interventions: Depending on the cause, specific treatments might be recommended to address the underlying issue.
