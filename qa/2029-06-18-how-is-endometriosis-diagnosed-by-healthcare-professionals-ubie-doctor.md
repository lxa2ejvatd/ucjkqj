Endometriosis is a condition where tissue similar to the lining inside the uterus grows outside of it. Diagnosing endometriosis can be challenging because symptoms can vary widely.

[diagnosed by](https://antimicrobial-hub.sunsimiaosd.com/)

Steps to Diagnose Endometriosis:

Symptom Evaluation: Doctors first ask about symptoms, such as pain and menstrual irregularities.

[How is endometriosis diagnosed](https://memorycircuits.hbsnzj.com/)

Imaging Tests: Ultrasounds or MRIs can help visualize the reproductive organs, but they cannot confirm endometriosis.

Surgical Diagnosis: The most definitive way to diagnose endometriosis is through a procedure called laparoscopy. This is a minor surgery where a camera is inserted into the pelvis to look for endometrial tissue.

Doctors use these methods to understand the extent of the condition and decide on the best treatment options. While imaging tests can suggest endometriosis, surgery is often needed to be sure.
