Can thyroid medication be taken with breakfast?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Yes, liquid and soft gel thyroid medications (such as levothyroxine in gel cap or oral solution form) can generally be taken with food, including breakfast, without a significant loss of effectiveness. Unlike traditional tablet formulations, which require an empty stomach for optimal absorption, these newer forms bypass many of the absorption issues caused by food, coffee, or calcium and iron supplements.

Key points to know:

[Thyroid Medication With Breakfast](https://benpepsi.com/)

Tablet thyroid medication typically requires taking it 30–60 minutes before eating for full absorption.

Liquid and soft gel forms are less affected by food and beverages, offering more flexibility with timing.

Always confirm with your prescribing doctor before changing how or when you take thyroid medication.

Thyroid medication, such as levothyroxine, is often taken to help manage thyroid hormone levels in the body. Traditionally, it has been recommended to take this medication on an empty stomach to ensure proper absorption. However, recent studies have shown that liquid and soft gel forms of thyroid medication can be taken with food, including breakfast, without affecting their effectiveness.

Key Points:

Liquid Formulation: Studies indicate that the liquid form of levothyroxine is absorbed well even when taken with meals. This can offer more flexibility for patients who find it difficult to take medication on an empty stomach.

Soft Gel Formulation: Similar to the liquid form, soft gel capsules are also shown to maintain their efficacy when consumed with food.

These findings can be particularly beneficial for patients who have difficulty adhering to fasting requirements.
