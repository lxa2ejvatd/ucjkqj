The temporomandibular joint connects your jawbone to your skull and helps with chewing and speaking. When this joint or the muscles around it are inflamed or strained (a condition called TMJ disorder), it can cause various symptoms including jaw pain, headaches, and sometimes tingling or numbness in the face.

Facial tingling in TMJ disorders is often caused by pressure on nearby nerves or muscle tension that affects nerve signals. Other symptoms may include jaw clicking, limited jaw movement, or soreness around the ear and face.

[Ubie Doctor's Note](https://cortexcompass.jcesqc8.com/)

If you experience persistent facial tingling along with jaw pain or difficulty moving your jaw, it’s a good idea to see a healthcare provider or dentist who specializes in TMJ disorders for evaluation and treatment options.
