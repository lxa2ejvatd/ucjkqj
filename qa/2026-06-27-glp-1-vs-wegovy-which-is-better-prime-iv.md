What Is GLP-1?

GLP-1 is a compound that mimics two different types of hormones. One is a glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1), and the other is a glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide (GIP). These are both hormones that your body releases naturally after you eat. By mimicking them, GLP-1 is able to mimic the effects they have on your body. This makes them a type of treatment that uses your body’s natural abilities to enhance it, like peptide therapy. GLP-1 is also the generic name for the medication. You may know it as Mounjaro, a brand name for it.

What Is Wegovy?

Wegovy is the brand name for the medication semaglutide. Semaglutide also goes by the brand names Ozempic and Rybelsus. The semaglutide in Wegovy is similar to GLP-1 in that it has an impact on hormones. Specifically, it is able to mimic GLP-1 hormones and, thus, produce a similar effect. However, unlike GLP-1, semaglutide cannot mimic GIP hormones.

What Do These Hormones Do?

In your body, hormones are essentially signals or messages your body sends to itself. They are how different parts of your body indicate that something needs to be accomplished somewhere. For example, hormones may be released when you are hurt so that your body can respond to the injury. GLP-1 and Wegovy impact specific hormones: GLP-1 and GIP. These hormones both have a purpose that relates to the goal of these medicines.

What Do GLP-1 Hormones Do?

Your body releases GLP-1 hormones naturally when you eat. These hormones make their way to your brain, where they send the signal that you are full and, thus, no longer need more food. Your brain then removes your desire for more food by making you feel full. This is all done to prevent overeating. Alongside this, GLP-1 slows gastric emptying. This means that it makes the food in your digestive system stay in the system for longer. This causes you to stay full for longer, as your body has not finished digesting what you just ate. However, there is some evidence that some people do not produce enough GLP-1. This means that their brains don’t get these same signals and, thus, don’t send the signal to stop eating. This causes someone with this condition to overeat. This is where products like GLP-1 and Wegovy are helpful. They help provide the levels of GLP-1 that your body needs to prevent overeating. Alongside other weight loss treatments, they can help someone lose weight and keep it off.

GIP assists GLP-1 in sending the signals that make you feel full. Together, these hormones work in harmony toward the same goal.

GLP-1 vs. Wegovy: FDA Approval

In 2021, the FDA approved Wegovy as a treatment for chronic weight management. This is significant, as this approval marked the first time the FDA approved a treatment like this since 2014. Meanwhile, GLP-1 has not been approved in a similar way. Mounjaro, the name brand for GLP-1, has been approved for treating diabetes. However, it has not been approved for weight management.
