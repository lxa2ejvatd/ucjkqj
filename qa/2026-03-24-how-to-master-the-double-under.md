# How to Master the Double-Under

[How to Master](https://matrix-skin.xianfengchem.com/blog/2562496052.html)

Double-unders or “dubs”—two passes under the jump rope—are a staple in CrossFit training. They’re a triple threat, challenging your cardio, endurance, and mind-muscle connection all at once.

[the Double-Under](https://focus-and-calm.sunsimiaosd.com/)

And they’re also hard as hell, requiring a ton of practice and patience. You won’t string together multiple flawless dubs on your first attempt.

That’s why we asked 2015 CrossFit Games champion Ben Smith to give us his best cues for mastering dubs. Check them out in the video above.

Then try The Flight Simulator—a double-under workout from James Fitzgerald, winner of the inaugural 2007 Games.

Or give 16.2—the second workout in the Reebok CrossFit Open—a shot. It calls for rounds of 50 dubs in the middle of the routine.
