Many things can make the scrotum itch. One common reason is a mild skin irritation from sweat, clothing, or tight rubbing, which can hurt the skin. Fungal infections like jock itch, which is caused by fungus that thrives in warm and moist conditions, can also cause itching. Allergies or contact dermatitis may happen if the skin touches something that irritates it. Other reasons can include skin conditions like eczema or even bug bites. It is important to keep the area clean and dry. If the itching continues or gets worse, you should talk to a healthcare provider to find out the exact cause and the best treatment for your situation.
