Explanation

Frequent urination is when someone needs to pee more often than usual. According to the studies, women tend to experience this more than men.

Why is this?

Overactive Bladder: Women are more likely to have an overactive bladder, which can make them feel like they need to go to the bathroom a lot.

Differences in Anatomy: Women's bodies are different from men's, which can also affect how often they need to urinate.

What Do Studies Say?

One study found that healthy women have different urination frequency ranges than men.

Another study used 24-hour urinary diaries and found that women reported more frequent urination than men.

Understanding these differences can help in managing and treating conditions related to frequent urination. If you're experiencing unusual urinary patterns, consider using a free symptom checker to better understand your symptoms and when to seek medical care.
