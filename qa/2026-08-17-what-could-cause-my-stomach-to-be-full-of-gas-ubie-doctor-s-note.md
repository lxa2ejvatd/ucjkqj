Explanation

Feeling like your stomach is full of gas can be uncomfortable and happens for various reasons. One common cause is irritable bowel syndrome (IBS). IBS can make your tummy feel bloated and gassy. It is a condition that affects how your stomach and intestines work.

Here are some reasons why you might feel gassy:

Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS): IBS can cause bloating and gas. People with IBS often feel their stomach is full of air.

Certain Foods: Some foods can cause more gas, like beans, broccoli, and carbonated drinks.

Swallowing Air: Sometimes, when you eat or drink quickly, you might swallow extra air, which can make your stomach feel gassy.

To help with gas, you can try eating slowly, avoiding foods that make you gassy, and talking to a doctor if it keeps happening. Remember, it’s always good to ask a doctor if you’re worried about your stomach.
